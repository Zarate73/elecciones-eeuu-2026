Where this race stands
Verified

Democrat Roy Cooper and Republican Michael Whatley are running for North Carolina's U.S. Senate seat in the November 3, 2026 general election. Neither is an incumbent. The race is rated Likely Democratic, and polls give Cooper a 9.25-point margin. The most recent poll is from SurveyUSA. It surveyed 643 likely voters, finished fieldwork on October 11 and shows Cooper leading Whatley 54% to 40% [1]. In prediction markets, a Democratic win is priced at 96% on both PredictIt [6] and Manifold [7]. Cooper is priced at 95.8% on Polymarket [8] and 95.1% on Kalshi [9].
