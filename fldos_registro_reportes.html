Voter Registration Reports

Webpage last updated: October 12, 2026

This page contains reports on current and historical voter registration statistical data. Data prior to January 2006 came directly from the county Supervisors of Elections. Data from January 2006 to the present are retrieved directly from the Florida Voter Registration System (FVRS) based on codes recorded in the system. Historical reports from 1995-2024 can be found under Archived Monthly Reports.

Current Voter Registration Data

Data below is current as of September 30, 2026.

  • Voter Registration - By County and Party (year-to-date) – Total number of ACTIVE registered voters by county and party affiliation
    • Monthly report by County and Party - Excel (Includes net differences month-to-month within a calendar year.  Note: The number of registered voters who have become inactive pursuant to s. 98.065, F.S., may account for negative net differences.)
  • Voter Registration - By Party Affiliation (year-to-date) – Total number of active registered voters by party affiliation
  • Voter Registration - By Source (Method and/or Location) and County (year-to-date) – Total number of voter registration applications received by source (method and location)
    • Monthly report by Source and County - Excel (includes each month within calendar year and all records whose registration date is within the month reported)
  • Voter Registration - New and Removed Voters by County (year-to-date) – Total number of new voters added and the number of active and inactive registered voters removed
    • Monthly report New and Removed Voters by County - Excel (includes each month within calendar year)
