Where this race stands
Verified

Democrat David Crowley and Republican Tom Tiffany face off for Wisconsin governor on November 3, 2026. The race rates lean-D with a polling-derived margin of 4 points. The most recent poll, from Marquette Law School (fielded through August 26, n=738 likely voters), shows Crowley leading Tiffany 49%–44% [N]. Prediction markets reinforce the Democratic advantage: Polymarket prices Crowley's win probability at 82.5% [4] and Kalshi at 80.0% [5].
