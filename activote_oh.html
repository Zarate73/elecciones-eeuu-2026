ActiVote’s October 19, 2026 Ohio Governor poll finds that Republican Vivek Ramaswamy leads Democrat Amy Acton by 2.8%. The poll was in the field between September 27 and October 19 (median field date of October 8). The poll was among 400 likely midterm voters and has an average expected error of 4.9%.

The headline result
51.4%
48.6%
Vivek Ramaswamy Amy Acton

Ramaswamy leads among rural and suburban voters, voters under 65, men, White voters, voters on high incomes, voters with no more than a high school education and college-educated voters. Acton leads among urban voters, voters aged 65 and older, women, Black voters, voters on low and middle incomes and graduate degree holders. These group figures rest on subsamples for which the expected error is considerably larger than the 4.9% for the poll as a whole. Ohio does not register voters by party, so we do not report a breakdown by party affiliation.

The two candidates hold their own sides about equally firmly. Acton takes all of the left and 84% of the moderate left, while Ramaswamy takes 93% of the right and 89% of the moderate right. The race is decided in the center, where Ramaswamy leads by 62% to 38%.
