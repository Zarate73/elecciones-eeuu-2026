Methodology
The On Point average is a robust LOWESS fit through every public poll, rebuilt for every day: each poll is weighted by recency with a 15.8 day half life, sample size, voter screen and pollster grade, a local line is fit through time and read at the day itself, and three bisquare robustness passes weigh down polls far from the rest. Rasmussen Reports and ActiVote are left out, and the measured lean of Trafalgar and InsiderAdvantage, to the right, and Impact Research, to the left, is taken off their polls in the average while the tables show them as published. On Point Polling polls are fielded by On Point Polling. How the average works.
