Where this race stands
Verified

Amy Acton (D) and Vivek Ramaswamy (R) face off for the Ohio governorship on November 3, 2026. The race rates lean-D with a polling-derived margin of 3.9 points. The most recent poll, conducted by Beacon Research/Shaw & Co. Research (field end October 19, n=2,005 registered voters), shows Acton leading Ramaswamy 51%–47% [2]. Prediction markets give Acton an implied win probability of 80% on Kalshi [6] and 78.5% on Polymarket [7].
