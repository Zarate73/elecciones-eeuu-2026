Data Files Documentation

Ranked Choice Voting contests are tabulated by the District of Columbia Board of Elections (DCBOE) using RCTab. RCTab produces summary results in two file formats: JSON and CSV. DCBOE uses the summary results files outputted by RCTab to produce RCV results reports and visualizations. Results visualizations were created using RCVis. All data in results reports and visualizations published by DCBOE is derived from RCTab summary data.
